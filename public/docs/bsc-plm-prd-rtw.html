Section 01

Purpose and scope

Requirements for the RTW edition of BSC PLM: Bombay Shirt Company's ready-to-wear bulk production, from the day category raises a style to the day it is in Enigma and the invoices are reconciled. Every role works on one record per style: a rail of steps down the left, one step open at a time, the next move and whose it is pinned in the header. The prototype at bsc-plm-rtw.vercel.app shows every screen named here.

BSC PLM sits beside Enigma, not inside it. Enigma stays the system of record for products, stock, warehouse receipts (GRN) and vendor POs for cloth. PLM holds what Enigma does not: the requirement and its files, the factory database, counter sourcing and its replies, the factory PO and its mails, the TNA with planned, revised and actual dates, QC, the cloth at the factory and the cut ratio, the invoice trail, COGS, and the conversation. AWB, courier and logistics stay outside PLM.

In scope. Category's requirement with its files; sourcing's vendor details and sending; the factory and cloth vendor databases; counter sourcing with replies over target flagged and target revisions logged; the factory PO with delivery location and the TNA form, mailed from PLM with the mail editable; the merchandiser's follow-ups across their factories; the TNA and QC; the cloth at the factory, colour by colour, with hold or drop on a failed colour and a new cut ratio on short or extra cloth or trims; dispatch with the invoice and packing list mailed to the warehouse; receipt against the packing list from Enigma; COGS; Add to Enigma with every size; invoices, debit and credit notes; the production dashboard.

Where it starts. PLM starts at the design requirement: once the concept is finalised, the prototype and sampling are done, and category knows the style is to be built. From that point, everything to the style being in Enigma and its invoices reconciled is in PLM.

Conventions. A state is where a record is, never the next step. Quantities are pieces unless the unit says metres. Rules marked later are specified but not built. A ✦ marks an AI feature.

Section 13

Business rules and validations

  • Nothing goes to Production RTW until every part is complete; sourcing's part only when BSC is in the cloth.
  • A reply over target cost or landing past the target in-house date is flagged; when none fits, category revises the target with a reason, and the earlier value is kept.
  • Who supplies the cloth is chosen by category on the requirement and confirmed by Nitin before the PO.
  • Delivery location: semi-stitched products to Vasai, everything else to the 3PL warehouse; category can choose either.
  • When BSC sends the cloth, the TNA's Fabric PO and Yarn PO blocks are not needed.
  • A failed colour is held (same PO) or dropped (separate requirement for the alternative); the other colours continue.
  • Short or extra cloth or trims, or a dropped colour, asks category for a new cut ratio.
  • A QC pass needs every checklist line; a fail needs remarks; a pass closes the TNA block.
  • Every mail PLM composes can be edited before sending; PLM keeps what was sent.
  • Each merchandiser sees only their own factories.
  • No Enigma auto-PO on RTW; no AWB, courier or logistics in PLM.
