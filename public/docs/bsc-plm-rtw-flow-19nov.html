BSC PLM · RTW flow · as built on 19 Nov 2026

1 · Requirement and vendors on the BOM

flowchart TD
A([Category raises a style]) --> B{One by one or bulk?}
B -- one by one --> C[Category fills the parts: ask, fabric, tech pack, trim card, sizes]
B -- bulk sheet --> B1{Row has a problem?}
B1 -- yes --> B2[Row skipped, shown in red]
B1 -- no --> C
C --> D[Category adds the BOM lines; quantities are optional here]
D --> E[Sourcing adds a vendor per line: supplied by, vendor, rate, GST, state]
E --> F{Every line has a vendor?}
F -- no --> E
F -- yes --> M{Several vendors on a line?}
M -- yes, optional --> G[Category picks one vendor per line, or the lowest rate on all]
M -- no, one vendor --> H
G --> H{Every part complete?}
H -- no --> H1[Gate lists what is missing, part by part]
H1 --> C
H -- yes --> I([Category sends to Production RTW])

2 · Factory counter sourcing

flowchart TD
A([Requirement arrives]) --> B[Nitin picks factories, the files each gets, and a deadline]
B --> C[PLM mails each factory its files and quote-form link]
C --> D{Factory fills the form by the deadline?}
D -- no --> D1[Turns red; on the merchandiser's follow-ups]
D1 --> D
D -- yes --> E[Reply lands: cost by head, cloth per piece and wastage, lead time, start date]
E --> F{Any reply within target cost and date?}
F -- no --> G[Category revises the target date or cost, with a reason]
G --> F
F -- yes --> H[Category completes the BOM with the real quantities from the quote and freezes them]
H --> H2[Category confirms the cloth route]
H2 --> I([To Enigma SKUs and the PO draft])

3 · Enigma SKUs, PO draft and approval

flowchart TD
A([Draft a PO: every query, its replies, the recommended factory]) --> B{SKUs in Enigma?}
B -- no --> B1[Category adds the style to Enigma]
B1 --> B
B -- yes --> C[Category ticks one or several requirements for one factory]
C --> C2[Category sees the drafted PO: every style a line, one total]
C2 --> D{Send for approval to?}
D -- both --> E[HOD and finance]
D -- only HOD --> E1[HOD]
D -- only finance --> E2[Finance]
D -- skip, with a reason --> K
E --> F{Decision}
E1 --> F
E2 --> F
F -- rejected, with a comment --> G[Category changes the PO and sends again]
G --> D
F -- no answer by the approve-by date --> F1[Flagged late on the approver's list]
F1 --> F
F -- everyone asked approved --> K[PLM raises the PO: one PO number for every style on it]
K --> L{Any BOM line sent by BSC?}
L -- yes --> M[PLM generates the tax invoice for that material]
L -- no --> N
M --> N([PLM mails the PO as one combined PDF, the TNA form and the invoice to the factory and the merchandiser])

4 · Cloth at the factory, cut ratio and changing a raised PO

flowchart TD
A([PO mailed]) --> B{TNA form back with planned dates?}
B -- no --> B1[On the merchandiser's follow-ups]
B1 --> B
B -- yes --> C{Who supplies the cloth?}
C -- BSC sends --> C1[Sourcing sends cloth and trims; factory counts them in]
C -- nominated vendor --> C2[Factory raises its own fabric PO and logs the receipt]
C -- factory's own --> C2
C1 --> D{Each colour: counted and checked}
C2 --> D
D -- passed, enough --> P([Into production])
D -- failed --> E{Nitin decides}
E -- hold --> E1[Replacement by a date, same PO]
E1 --> D
E -- drop --> R[Category gives new sizes, or approves a cut-short]
D -- short or extra cloth or trims --> Q[Factory or merchandiser asks for a new cut ratio]
Q --> R
R --> S[Category changes the PO; new sizes filled in]
S --> T{Approval again: HOD, finance or both}
T -- rejected --> S
T -- approved --> U[Revision number goes up; revised PO mailed by PLM]
U --> P

6 · Dispatch, count-in and COGS

flowchart TD
A([Dispatch logged]) --> B[Merchandiser uploads the invoice and the packing list PDF with its pieces]
B --> C[PLM mails both to the warehouse]
C --> W{Where did the goods land?}
W -- Vasai --> D[Prince counts in by size against the PO]
W -- the 3PL --> D2[Merchandiser uploads the 3PL's consignment report; the count by size fills from it]
D --> E{Everything received?}
D2 --> E
E -- yes --> H[Received in full]
E -- damaged pieces --> E1[Entered with a note]
E1 --> E
E -- short, more coming --> F[Balance expected on a date]
F --> W
E -- short, no more coming --> G[Closed short; missing pieces come off the payable]
G --> H
H --> I[Direct COGS closed: PO cost plus fixed packaging, shipping and process costs]
I --> I2[Cost x multiplier = MRP, checked against the MRP in Enigma]
I2 --> J[To finance: payments, in parts]
I2 --> M[Category: Enigma and marketing details, on its own screen]

7 · Finance: approve the PO, add payments

flowchart TD
A([PO drafted by category and sent for approval]) --> B{Finance asked to approve?}
B -- no, only HOD --> K[PO raised on the HOD's approval]
B -- yes --> C{Finance's decision}
C -- rejected, with a comment --> C1[Back to category to change and send again]
C1 --> A
C -- approved --> K2[PO raised and mailed by PLM]
K --> P[Payment made to the factory, in full or in part, outside PLM]
K2 --> P
P --> R{How is it added?}
R -- one payment --> R1[On the PO: date, amount, reference, note]
R -- many at once --> R2[Upload a sheet; each row lands on its PO]
R2 --> R3{PO number found and an amount?}
R3 -- no --> R4[Row skipped, shown in red]
R3 -- yes --> S
R1 --> S{Paid equals the net payable?}
S -- no: the PO shows total, paid and remaining --> P
S -- yes --> L([PO paid in full])
