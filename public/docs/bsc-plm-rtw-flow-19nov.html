1 · Requirement and vendors on the BOM

flowchart TD
A(["Category raises a style"]) --> B{"One by one or bulk sheet?"}
B -- "bulk sheet" --> B1{"PLM checks each row:
style, category, target cost, date"}
B1 -- "a problem" --> B2["Row skipped, shown in red"]
B1 -- "fine" --> C
B -- "one by one" --> C["1 · Style and production ask
category, sub-category, style name, colour options
approximate order qty, target in-house date, target cost per piece
semi-stitched? → delivers to Vasai, else the 3PL
files: style sheet, references; the design brief"]
C --> C2["2 · Fabric information
description, content, photo of the physical fabric
option and phase (swatch analyser), CPM, vendor rate, width"]
C2 --> C3["3 · Tech pack · 12 items, each a file or a note
sketch, styling detail, stitch dimensions, colourways, spec sheet,
how to measure, folding, trim card, label, hang tag, barcodes, packaging"]
C3 --> C4["4 · Trim card
the file; per colour: trim, supplier item code, reference image"]
C4 --> D["5 · BOM · pre-filled from the category template
per line: item, description, colour, qty per piece, unit, wastage %
quantities optional now: category standards stand in as an estimate"]
D --> E["Sourcing adds per BOM line:
supplied by (BSC sends · nominated vendor · factory),
vendor, rate, GST %, vendor state"]
E --> F{"Every line has a vendor?"}
F -- "no" --> E
F -- "yes" --> M{"Several vendors on a line?"}
M -- "yes, optional" --> G["Category picks one per line, or the lowest rate on all"]
M -- "no" --> H
G --> H{"Category's half complete?"}
H -- "no" --> H1["Gate lists what is missing, part by part"]
H1 --> C
H -- "yes" --> J{"Planning's sizes in?"}
J -- "not yet" --> J1["On Charanjeet's Sizes to add list
shows category's ask (about N pcs) and the target date"]
J1 --> K{"Category: wait, or start counter sourcing now?"}
K -- "start now" --> I2(["To counter sourcing on a per-piece quote;
only the PO waits for the sizes"])
K -- "wait" --> J2["Charanjeet adds the size-wise quantities
quantity per size (e.g. 38: 30 · 40: 60 · 42: 60 · 44: 30)
order qty = the sum; this is the cut ratio on the PO"]
J2 --> J
J -- "yes" --> I(["Category sends to Production RTW"])

2 · Factory counter sourcing

flowchart TD
A(["Requirement arrives, with or without its sizes"]) --> B["Nitin picks factories from the factory database
recommended by the category's specialisation and on-time rate
per factory: which files it gets · a reply deadline"]
B --> C["PLM mails each factory its files and a quote-form link
(no login), the merchandiser copied"]
C --> D{"Factory fills the form by the deadline?"}
D -- "no" --> D1["Turns red; on the merchandiser's follow-ups"]
D1 --> D
D -- "yes" --> E["Reply lands by itself
cost per piece by head (CMT, cloth, trims, packing, other)
cloth consumption per piece, wastage %
lead time in days, start date, a note"]
E --> F{"Any reply within target cost and in-house date?"}
F -- "no: every reply over" --> G["Category revises the target cost or date, with a reason
(the record keeps what it was, who and when)"]
G --> F
F -- "yes" --> H["Category completes the BOM from the quote
consumption and wastage → quantity per piece and for N pcs
then freezes it for costing (a later change is a revision)"]
H --> H2["Category confirms the cloth route:
BSC sends · factory buys from BSC's vendor · factory's own"]
H2 --> I(["To Enigma SKUs and the PO draft"])

3 · Enigma SKUs, PO draft and approval

flowchart TD
A(["Draft a PO: every query, its replies,
the factory recommended on price and in-house date"]) --> B{"SKUs in Enigma?"}
B -- "no" --> B1["Category adds the style to Enigma
design code, name it sells as, fit, colour, description
every size: SKU, price, sale price, pcs
MRP shown beside: direct cost × multiplier"]
B1 --> B
B1 -.-> MK["Marketing fills alongside
title, description, buzzwords, key features,
fabric and care, fit notes, SEO title, shoot pointers"]
MK -.-> MK2(["Go-live waits for these; the PO never does"])
B -- "yes" --> SZ{"Sizes in from Charanjeet?"}
SZ -- "no" --> SZ1["Shown as sizes with Charanjeet; cannot be ticked"]
SZ1 --> SZ
SZ -- "yes" --> C["Category ticks one or several requirements for one factory"]
C --> C2["PLM drafts the PO: every style a line, one total
per colour: qty per size × rate · other charges (logistics)
taxable value, GST, PO total · ship-by, goods expected by
payment terms (advance %), raw material terms, tolerance, AQL"]
C2 --> D{"Send for approval to?"}
D -- "both" --> E["HOD and finance"]
D -- "only HOD" --> E1["HOD"]
D -- "only finance" --> E2["Finance"]

E2 --> F

F -- "everyone asked approved" --> K["PLM raises the PO: one PO number for every style on it"]
K --> N["PLM mails the PO as one combined PDF and the TNA form
to the factory and the merchandiser, with the delivery location"]
N --> L{"Any BOM line sent by BSC?"}
L -- "yes" --> M(["PLM writes a transfer note for that material; it goes to Desai"])
L -- "no" --> N2(["No transfer note"])

4 · Material BSC sends, cloth at the factory, cut ratio and changing a raised PO

flowchart TD
A(["PO mailed"]) --> B{"TNA form back with planned dates?"}
B -- "no" --> B1["On the merchandiser's follow-ups"]
B1 --> B
B -- "yes" --> C{"Who supplies the cloth?"}

the rest is the factory's"]
T4 -- "factory buys from a vendor" --> T9["PLM splits the BOM line:
the rest nominated, vendor and rate"]
T4 -- "BSC buys from another vendor" --> T6["PLM splits the BOM line:
the rest BSC's, vendor and rate"]
T6 -.-> T10["When it is in, a new transfer note for it"]
T10 -.-> T1
T8 --> T5
T9 --> T5

U --> P

6 · Dispatch, count-in and COGS

flowchart TD
A(["Dispatch logged on the TNA"]) --> B["Merchandiser uploads the factory's invoice (number, amount, file)
and the packing list PDF with its piece count"]
B --> C["PLM mails both to the warehouse"]
C --> W{"Where did the goods land?"}
W -- "Vasai (semi-stitched)" --> D["Prince counts in by size against the PO"]
W -- "the 3PL" --> D2["Merchandiser uploads the 3PL's consignment report"]
D2 --> D3["PLM fills the count by size from the report"]
D --> E{"Everything received?"}
D3 --> E
E -- "yes" --> H["Received in full"]
E -- "damaged pieces" --> E1["Entered with a note"]
E1 --> E
E -- "short, more coming" --> F["Balance expected on a date"]
F --> W
E -- "short, no more coming" --> G["Closed short"]
G --> G2["PLM takes the missing pieces × rate off the payable"]
G2 --> H
H --> I["Direct COGS per piece
PO cost (FOB per piece) + other charges on the PO per piece
+ fixed packaging + fixed shipping + fixed process costs
e.g. ₹300 FOB + ₹10 logistics + ₹44 packaging + ₹142 shipping + ₹113 process = ₹609
(fixed costs per piece by category: BSC to confirm)"]
I --> I2["MRP by the multiplier
direct COGS × multiplier (category's, e.g. 3.5)
₹609 × 3.5 = ₹2,131.50, rounded up to the next hundred less ₹10 → MRP ₹2,190
checked against the MRP in Enigma; margin shown"]
I2 --> J["To finance: payments, in parts"]
I2 --> M{"Marketing details done?"}
M -- "no" --> M1["On marketing's list, red if it lands within 3 weeks"]
M1 --> M
M -- "yes" --> M2(["Ready to go live"])
class C,W,D3,E,G2,I,I2,M,M1 auto

7 · Finance: approve the PO, add payments

flowchart TD
A(["PO drafted by category and sent for approval"]) --> B{"Finance asked to approve?"}
B -- "no, only HOD" --> K["PO raised on the HOD's approval"]
B -- "yes" --> C{"Finance's decision
sees the PO, every reply, past prices for the category"}
C -- "rejected, with a comment" --> C1["Back to category to change and send again"]
C1 --> A
C -- "approved" --> K2["PO raised and mailed by PLM"]
K --> NP
K2 --> NP["Net payable on the PO
PO total (incl. GST and other charges)
− BSC's invoices for material Desai sent
− debit notes − pieces not received × rate"]
NP --> P["Payment made to the factory, in full or in part, outside PLM
advance % due up front where the terms say so"]
P --> R{"How is it added?"}
R -- "one payment" --> R1["Finance adds it on the PO: date, amount, reference, note"]
R -- "many at once" --> R2["Finance uploads a sheet"]
R2 --> R5["Each row lands on its PO"]
R5 --> R3{"PO number found and an amount?"}
R3 -- "no" --> R4["Row skipped, shown in red"]
R3 -- "yes" --> S
R1 --> S{"Paid equals the net payable?"}
S -- "no: the PO shows total, paid and remaining (paid ₹400 of ₹1,000 → ₹600 remains)" --> P
S -- "yes" --> L(["PO paid in full"])
