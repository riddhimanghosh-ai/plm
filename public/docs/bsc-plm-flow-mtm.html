BSC PLM — MTM edition, the flow

20 Oct 2026 · Riddhiman Ghosh · DevX Labs · prototype at plm-xi-green.vercel.app · PRD at bsc-plm-prd-mtm.vercel.app

The made-to-measure business as boxes and arrows, in the order a shirt happens: the customer's order, the day's production and the factory floor, then the cloth behind it. Every box names the team that does it. BSC PLM sits beside Enigma: it reads fabrics, orders, POs and stock from Enigma, keeps everything Enigma does not hold, and writes back where access is agreed. Nothing ready-to-wear is on this page.
