1. Purpose and scope

This is the requirements document for the MTM edition of BSC PLM: the version of the tool that covers Bombay Shirt Company's made-to-measure business end to end and nothing else. It is what the MTM prototype (VITE_EDITION=mtm, http://localhost:5176) shows, written down screen by screen so the delivery team can build from it. The workflows run in the order a shirt happens: the customer's order at the store, the day's production and the factory floor, then the cloth behind it — the fabric master, purchase orders, sourcing, the design loop and the swatch desk. The full prototype remains the reference for the ready-to-wear work; everything that is RTW-only is out of this document and comes in a later phase.

BSC PLM is built beside Enigma, not inside it. Enigma stays the system of record for fabrics, vendors, customer orders, purchase orders and stock. BSC PLM reads what it needs over Enigma's API and keeps its own records for everything Enigma does not hold: the design loop on a new fabric, the vendor's terms and hold, the swatch desk, production plans, scans, changed sheets, cases and conversation. Where BSC PLM shows a field Enigma owns, it shows it under Enigma's own name, so what is typed here is what goes there.

In scope. The customer's order first: the store's MTM work (bills, fit profiles, trial and express, sheet corrections, returns, credit, refunds); MTM daily production (queue, plan, floor, changed sheets, extra fabric, ship-by moves), the factory merchandiser's MTM floor and the Factories screen. Then the cloth: the MTM fabric master as Enigma's fabric DB (fabric info, attributes, inventory, transactions, reservations); purchase orders, raised by Enigma or by hand, and the receiving line; the core weekly stock review; the sourcing desk and the vendor master; the MTM fabric requirement from ask to live online (five states); the swatch desk up to the handover to Yusuf; and the conversation and files on every record; and the cross-cutting bell and names rule.

Out of this edition. Everything RTW: style requests, planning review, launches, the production tracker and TNA, stock health, OTIF. "Several options at once" requests are retired: design raises each option as its own MTM fabric ask. FOB is retired: BSC buys the cloth. The warehouse portal (vehicle round, RTW lots, STOs, books, hemming round trip, GRN register) is held for a later phase; in this edition fabric receiving happens on the purchase order's receiving line and the swatch desk stops at the handover. Bespoke's daily mill list and parcel inward return with the warehouse portal; the store-side gate on sold-out bespoke cloth stays.

Conventions. Dates are the record's own. Quantities are pieces unless the unit says metres. A state is where a record IS, never the next step. Every rule marked later is specified but not built in the prototype. Field names in italics are Enigma's.

5. Workflow 2 — MTM daily production and the factory floor

The production unit runs its day from one screen: Allocate, Plans, Factory floor, Changed sheets, Extra fabric. The system proposes the plan from each factory's spare capacity and a person confirms it. Every move of a piece is a scan. Daily output, aging and the capacity inputs sit on the Factories screen.

Stages, in order. Order (punched at the store) → PP (waiting for a factory; frozen 2 hours for store-side changes) → PP · planned (on a plan; job card printed; fabric cut) → In production (scanned out of the unit) → In factory (scanned in; aging after 5 days) → Out factory (QC passed) → RTS (back at the unit) → Fitting / shipping → Delivered. Cancelled can happen before cutting.

The rule. Saleable = physical − reserved − floor. Rate = shirts a week over 8 weeks. Metres a week = rate × 2.15. Lead-time demand = metres a week × lead days ÷ 7. Reorder point = the larger of lead-time demand and the vendor's MOQ. Under it when saleable + on order ≤ the point. PO = the MOQ. Never for bespoke. Promotion to core lifts the floor to 40 m if under 20.

9. Workflow 6 — Design: an MTM fabric, requirement to live online

The record opens on four sections in that order; there is no section for "PO punched" because it is a state, not work.

12. Cross-cutting behaviour

The bell · Waiting on you. One bell per person showing their team's items, grouped by the job in plain words ("Give a new cut ratio", "Raise the purchase order", "Add a vendor") with a count and how many are urgent. Each line names the record, the workflow it belongs to (MTM fabric ask, Swatch desk, Store floor, MTM production, Fabric master…), where the record stands now ("2 of 5 · Vendor added"), why it is on the list, and an Open button to where the job is done. Lines are recomputed from the records on every change and disappear the moment the record moves. Messages (mentions) are the second tab.

Sidebar. Teams are pills with a role-coloured dot and a count; a pill opens to its screens on a rail. The team you are in is marked. Saved views keep a screen with its filters by name.

Records. A fabric is a page with a facts strip and tabs; a mill, a requirement and an order open as drawers whose sections open scrolled to their heading. Every record carries files and a conversation with mentions (workflow 8); nothing is emailed, the record is the thread.

Names. A fabric, a design and a launch each carry a working name, a final name and a history of every earlier name with who, when and why. The code is the identity; analytics key on the code and show the current name.

Who acts next. Every record header carries one state. A button appears only for the role that owns the step; others see whose move it is. Fields that can be edited look editable: dashed tiles, a pencil on hover, a chip that says so.

Colour. One accent (green) for the thing to click: primary buttons, the active tab, the hovered row, the sidebar's current screen. Four state tones with a border so they read at a glance: green good or live, amber waiting on you, blue on its way, red a problem. The five requirement states keep one colour each everywhere they appear: grey requirement requested, amber vendor added, blue PO punched, green-tint fabric in, green live online. Team colours (plum design, ochre sourcing, teal production, green store) mark whose hand a record is in, never a state.

Lists. Paged at 20 to 25; filters and the open record in the URL so a view is shareable and Back works; every list exports to .xlsx; search across requirements, fabrics, mills, orders, customers.

Undo and audit. The last ten writes can be undone for seven seconds by the person who made them. Every write appends who, when and what; order sheets keep versions V1, V2, V3 with from and to; ship-by moves keep the original date and the reason.

14. Business rules and validations

AreaRule
RequirementsName and why required. A variant's base fabric must be inventory, over 20 m, not bespoke, not paused or on hold. A requirement is in exactly one of five states; the state is derived, never typed.
Vendors and holdsOne live vendor per ask; a second replaces the first and is kept as replaced. Push PO refused on a lapsed hold until sourcing extends it. Reject needs a reason. Extend must be later than the old date (later). The PO amount follows the typed metres at the vendor's price.
Purchase ordersA PO raised by Enigma carries source auto; by a person, manual with who. Received metres never exceed pending; Close with metres pending cancels the balance and is marked closed short. Every receiving update raises a GRN. "Needs a PO by hand" is only international core under its reorder point with nothing on order.
DesignsA design needs a name and one variant with a price. Price is per variant; no design-level price. A price change on a design in Enigma re-pushes it. The product code is minted once.
NamesA rename appends the old name to the history with who, when and why; the code never changes.
Fabric masterEdits to Enigma's fields are saved under Enigma's names. A stock adjustment is signed metres with a reason and a description. Threshold on the inventory block is the reorder floor. One sell action per state.
Go live onlineBlocked until composition and a description are on the record; every other gate is advisory and the skips are kept. Stores are not a gate.
Swatch deskSteps in order; phase one ends at the handover to Yusuf.
MTM productionA piece joins the queue after the freeze window. A plan over headroom is refused unless forced, and forcing is recorded with the name. Urgent sets ship-by to punch + 2 days. Scan-out deducts metres from stock and reserved. A bespoke piece is never planned before its parcel is in. A ship-by move needs a reason.
Sheet changesStore: freely until allocation, remarks only after, nothing once made. Production: freely until planning; amendments flagged for reprint in transit or in factory. Each change makes the next version (V2, V3); the factory's held version is shown until re-issued.
StoreOnly live fabrics can be punched; a sold-out bespoke fabric cannot. Credit never above the balance; credits expire after a year. A case is raised only on a piece delivered from this store; within 30 days free alteration and credit note, after that chargeable. A full refund needs approval before finance moves it.
ReplenishmentReorder point = the larger of lead-time demand and MOQ; PO = the MOQ. Never for bespoke. Promotion to core lifts the floor to 40 m if under 20. A fabric the mill reports out goes on hold at once.
RolesA button appears only for the owning role. Store and factory roles are scoped to one store or factory. Owner sees and may do everything.
Conversation and filesOne thread per record, never deleted, readable by everyone who can open the record. A mention goes to a person or a team and stays on the bell until that person resolves it; resolving is kept on the comment. Files carry a label from the record kind's list; 25 MB per file. Nothing is emailed.

  • A change filter or feed on the fabric read; a date or code range on the transaction log.

  • Whether the warehouse books rolls on the PO line in BSC PLM in phase 3, or in Enigma with the number copied, until the portal arrives in phase 4.
  • The variant option set Enigma accepts (free text, or a fixed Size list) and whether a discounted price per variant is a field or a rule.
  • Who owns Big-Checked and composition family in Enigma today (fabric attribute or product flag) — this document puts them on the fabric.
  • Whether a required reason on Reject and a later-than-before check on Extend should block.
  • Bespoke: the promised date against the MTM default, and where the daily mill list lives until the warehouse portal (phase 4).
