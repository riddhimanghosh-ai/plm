Section 01

Purpose and scope

Requirements for the MTM edition of BSC PLM: Bombay Shirt Company's made-to-measure business end to end, as the MTM prototype shows it, screen by screen. Workflows run in the order a shirt happens: the order at the store, the day's production and the factory floor, then the cloth (fabric master, purchase orders, sourcing, the design loop, the swatch desk). RTW-only work is out and comes in a later phase; the full prototype stays its reference.

BSC PLM sits beside Enigma, not inside it. Enigma stays the system of record for fabrics, vendors, customer orders, purchase orders and stock. BSC PLM reads what it needs over Enigma's API and keeps its own records for what Enigma does not hold: the design loop, vendor terms and holds, the swatch desk, production plans, scans, changed sheets, cases and conversation. Fields Enigma owns appear under Enigma's own names, so what is typed here is what goes there.

In scope. The store's MTM work (bills, fit profiles, trial and express, sheet corrections, returns, credit, refunds); MTM daily production (queue, plan, pre-production desks, floor, changed sheets, extra material (fabric and trims), ship-by moves), the factory merchandiser's floor and the Factories screen; the MTM fabric master as Enigma's fabric DB; purchase orders (auto or by hand) and the receiving line; the core weekly stock review; the sourcing desk and vendor master; the fabric requirement from ask to live online (five states); the swatch desk up to the handover to Yusuf; conversation and files on every record; the bell and names rule.

Conventions. Dates are the record's own. Quantities are pieces unless the unit says metres. A state is where a record is, never the next step. Rules marked later are specified but not built in the prototype. Field names in italics are Enigma's.

Section 05 / Workflow 2

MTM daily production and the factory floor

Production runs its day from one screen: Allocate, Plans, Pre-production, Factory floor, Changed sheets, Extra material. The system proposes each plan from the factories' spare capacity; a person confirms. Every move of a piece is a scan. Daily output, aging and capacity inputs sit on the Factories screen.

Stages, in order. Order (punched) → PP (waiting for a factory; frozen 2 hours for store changes) → PP · planned (on a plan) → pre-production at the unit: CAD → Cutting → Fusing → Compiling → In production (scanned out) → In factory (aging after 5 days) → Out factory (QC passed) → RTS (back at the unit) → Fitting / shipping → Delivered. Cancelled is possible before cutting.

The rule. Saleable = physical − reserved − floor. Rate = shirts a week over 8 weeks. Metres a week = rate × 2.15. Lead-time demand = metres a week × lead days ÷ 7. Reorder point = the larger of lead-time demand and the vendor's MOQ; under it when saleable + on order ≤ the point. PO = the MOQ. Never for bespoke. Promotion to core lifts the floor to 40 m if under 20.

Across the top, one row of boxes for the whole journey: Requirement raised · Sampling · Vendor added · PO pushed · Fabric in · Details in Enigma · Swatches to Yusuf · Sent to stores · Live online. Each box ticks itself with the date and who when its step is done; the current one is highlighted. The boxes are the tabs: clicking one opens only that step's section below, the current step first, so the record does not scroll. Checklists inside a step run left to right. Files and conversation sit at the foot.

Trigger. A gap in a swatch book, a customer ask, or a variant worth making. Done when live online with its design in Enigma. Stores follow the swatch desk and do not gate going live online.

Section 12

Business rules and validations

AreaRule
RequirementsName and why required. A variant's base must be inventory, over 20 m, not bespoke, not paused or on hold. One of five states, derived, never typed. A new ask reaches sourcing only after design decides on sampling: not needed sends it at once; needed sends it once the approval is posted.
Vendors and holdsOne live vendor per ask; a second replaces the first, kept as replaced. Push PO refused on a lapsed hold until extended. Reject needs a reason. Extend must be later (later). PO amount = typed metres × the vendor's price.
Purchase ordersEnigma-raised is source auto; person-raised is manual with who. Received never exceeds pending; Close with metres pending cancels the balance as closed short. Every receiving update raises a GRN. Needs a PO by hand is only international core under its point with nothing on order.
DesignsOne product code per fabric, minted once by Save · send to Enigma. Name, MRP, product page text and consumption are filled once on the requirement and sent to Enigma in the same save; an edit re-syncs. Each size carries its own price, discounted price and SKU; the MRP is the lowest size price.
NamesA rename appends the old name with who, when and why; the code never changes.
Fabric masterEdits saved under Enigma's field names. An adjustment is signed metres with a reason and description. Threshold is the reorder floor. Inactive is set and cleared by hand only (Enigma's On Hold); out of stock is set by the system when saleable falls to the threshold.
Go live onlineLive online is the last box, after swatch photography, video and post-production are ticked. Stores are not a gate.
Swatch deskTick boxes in order, only the next one, by the warehouse; a ticked box keeps who and when and is not unticked. Phase one runs to the handover to Yusuf and then Sent to stores: one line per store, dispatched by the warehouse with a tracking no., received by the store in RetailOS. The desk list and the requirement show the same boxes.
Extra materialFabric or trims. Approval from the shelf deducts from physical stock at once, alteration and hemming cloth included, and trims from the trim's stock in pieces. Approve is refused when the shelf does not cover the metres or pieces (shown as low inventory). A decline needs a reason, which the factory reads. Only bespoke fabric, or a fabric shortfall, goes to Shravani in one click; trims never go to Shravani; she approves and arranges the cloth, and it returns to production when the cloth is in. Bespoke is never inventory: nothing booked in or deducted. A fuller tool for Swati comes later.
Washed fabricWashed is set on the requirement; shrinkage % is entered at receiving once a cut is washed. Only washed fabric shrinks: stock books received × (1 − shrinkage); the GRN keeps metres received.
Pre-confirmed ordersNever planned to a factory; shown only under the Pre-confirmed filter until confirmed.
Pre-productionEvery planned order passes CAD, cutting, fusing and compiling in order, each step recording who took it up, who finished and when. A block needs a reason. the CAD file is optional. Scan-out is refused until compiled.
Contrast fabricInventory fabric in small amounts; compiling approval deducts it from physical stock. Reserved is the main fabric held at punch; saleable = physical − reserved − floor.
MTM productionA piece joins the queue after the freeze. Over headroom is refused unless forced, and forcing is recorded with the name. Urgent sets ship-by to punch + 2 days. Scan-out deducts from stock and reserved. Bespoke is never planned before its parcel. A ship-by move needs a reason.
Sheet changesBefore production (order or PP) store and production can correct the sheet, each correction the next version (V2, V3). On a plan, measurements and styling lock and free-form remarks stop; only a logistics remark can change, and it is shown to the factory.
StoreOnly live fabrics can be punched; no sold-out bespoke. Credit never above the balance; credits expire after a year. Cases only on pieces delivered from this store; within 30 days free alteration and credit note, after that chargeable. A full refund needs approval before finance moves it.
ReplenishmentReorder point = the larger of lead-time demand and MOQ; PO = the MOQ. Never for bespoke. Promotion to core lifts the floor to 40 m if under 20. A fabric the mill reports out goes on hold at once.
RolesButtons only for the owning role. Store and factory roles are scoped to one store or factory. Owner sees and may do everything.
Conversation and filesOne thread per record, never deleted, readable by everyone who can open it. A mention stays on the bell until resolved; resolving is kept. Files carry a label per record kind; 25 MB each. Nothing is emailed.

Section 13

Non-functional requirements and the Enigma contract

AreaRequirement
ArchitectureA separate application beside Enigma: reads over its API, its own database for its records, write-back behind per-module flags in the last phase, nothing built in Enigma's codebase. One codebase builds both editions; MTM is a build flag hiding RTW screens and the warehouse portal.
Enigma readsFabric master (one 3.2 MB call, no change filter) with the fabric image: nightly and on demand. Inventory per warehouse with each bin's code, rack location and photo. Transaction and reservation logs per code until a date range is confirmed. Fabric POs with receiving lines. Orders and order sheets. Vendors. Designs and variants.
Enigma writes (last phase, if granted)Fabric info, attribute and inventory edits; On Hold when a fabric is made inactive, cleared when made active; the fabric PO at Push PO and Raise PO; Update Receiving Status; Add and Edit Vendor; design push with variants; order-sheet corrections. Until then each is keyed in Enigma and the number recorded here.
Other readsShopify sales by product code daily, and the customer profile. The order sheet XML from the connected order software. Mill lists pasted. Sheets imported once at go-live, idempotent, every skipped row logged.
IdentityOne login per person, one role, store or factory scope where it applies; an administrator sees everything.
ConcurrencyServer-side records with optimistic versioning; a stale write is refused and the current state shown; Enigma's reservation decides when two stores punch the last metres.
AuditEvery write appends who, when and what. Order sheets keep versions. Nothing hard-deleted. History exportable.
PerformanceServer-side filtering for the fabric master (about 8,600 rows) and orders (about 40 a day). The bell recomputed under a second on every write.
AvailabilityStores work 10:00 to 22:00 IST, seven days; maintenance outside those hours.
DevicesDesktop first; a narrow layout with large buttons for the returns bench and the swatch desk.
FilesObject storage, 25 MB per file, images previewed inline, label per record kind.
EnvironmentsDevelopment; staging reading Enigma read-only against production data; production. Enigma has no sandbox, so writes are flagged per module per environment.
Backups · localisationDaily, kept 90 days. English; dates as 12 Sep 26; rupees with Indian grouping; inches for measurements, metres for cloth.
Parallel runEach phase keeps the sheet it replaces read-only for one month, with a weekly comparison with the owning team before switch-off.
