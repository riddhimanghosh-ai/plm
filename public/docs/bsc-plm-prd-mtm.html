Section 01

Purpose and scope

This is the requirements document for the MTM edition of BSC PLM: the version of the tool that covers Bombay Shirt Company's made-to-measure business end to end and nothing else. It is what the MTM prototype shows, written down screen by screen so the delivery team can build from it. The workflows run in the order a shirt happens: the customer's order at the store, the day's production and the factory floor, then the cloth behind it, the fabric master, purchase orders, sourcing, the design loop and the swatch desk. The full prototype remains the reference for the ready-to-wear work; everything that is RTW-only is out of this document and comes in a later phase.

BSC PLM is built beside Enigma, not inside it. Enigma stays the system of record for fabrics, vendors, customer orders, purchase orders and stock. BSC PLM reads what it needs over Enigma's API and keeps its own records for everything Enigma does not hold: the design loop on a new fabric, the vendor's terms and hold, the swatch desk, production plans, scans, changed sheets, cases and conversation. Where BSC PLM shows a field Enigma owns, it shows it under Enigma's own name, so what is typed here is what goes there.

In scope. The customer's order first: the store's MTM work (bills, fit profiles, trial and express, sheet corrections, returns, credit, refunds); MTM daily production (queue, plan, floor, changed sheets, extra fabric, ship-by moves), the factory merchandiser's MTM floor and the Factories screen. Then the cloth: the MTM fabric master as Enigma's fabric DB (fabric info, attributes, inventory, transactions, reservations); purchase orders, raised by Enigma or by hand, and the receiving line; the core weekly stock review; the sourcing desk and the vendor master; the MTM fabric requirement from ask to live online (five states); the swatch desk up to the handover to Yusuf; and the conversation and files on every record; and the cross-cutting bell and names rule.

Conventions. Dates are the record's own. Quantities are pieces unless the unit says metres. A state is where a record IS, never the next step. Every rule marked later is specified but not built in the prototype. Field names in italics are Enigma's.

Section 05 / Workflow 2

MTM daily production and the factory floor

The production unit runs its day from one screen: Allocate, Plans, Factory floor, Changed sheets, Extra fabric. The system proposes the plan from each factory's spare capacity and a person confirms it. Every move of a piece is a scan. Daily output, aging and the capacity inputs sit on the Factories screen.

Stages, in order. Order (punched at the store) → PP (waiting for a factory; frozen 2 hours for store-side changes) → PP · planned (on a plan) → pre-production at the unit: CAD → Cutting → Fusing → Compiling → In production (scanned out of the unit) → In factory (scanned in; aging after 5 days) → Out factory (QC passed) → RTS (back at the unit) → Fitting / shipping → Delivered. Cancelled can happen before cutting.

The rule. Saleable = physical − reserved − floor. Rate = shirts a week over 8 weeks. Metres a week = rate × 2.15. Lead-time demand = metres a week × lead days ÷ 7. Section 09 / Workflow 6

Design: an MTM fabric, requirement to live online

One new made-to-measure fabric, shade or construction variant runs on one record. The record is always in exactly one of five states, and the list shows that state in one column: 1 Requirement requested (sourcing has to add a vendor) · 2 Vendor added (design has to punch the PO) · 3 PO punched (the cloth is on its way) · 4 Fabric in (design configures Enigma; design codes can be added at any stage) · 5 Live online. The record opens on four sections in that order; there is no section for "PO punched" because it is a state, not work.

Trigger. Design sees a gap in a swatch book, a customer ask, or a variant worth making. Done when the fabric is live online with its designs in Enigma. Stores follow the swatch desk and are not a gate for going live online.

Section 12

Business rules and validations

AreaRule
RequirementsName and why required. A variant's base fabric must be inventory, over 20 m, not bespoke, not paused or on hold. A requirement is in exactly one of five states; the state is derived, never typed.
Vendors and holdsOne live vendor per ask; a second replaces the first and is kept as replaced. Push PO refused on a lapsed hold until sourcing extends it. Reject needs a reason. Extend must be later than the old date (later). The PO amount follows the typed metres at the vendor's price.
Purchase ordersA PO raised by Enigma carries source auto; by a person, manual with who. Received metres never exceed pending; Close with metres pending cancels the balance and is marked closed short. Every receiving update raises a GRN. "Needs a PO by hand" is only international core under its reorder point with nothing on order.
DesignsA design needs a name and one variant with a price. Price is per variant; no design-level price. A price change on a design in Enigma re-pushes it. The product code is minted once.
NamesA rename appends the old name to the history with who, when and why; the code never changes.
Fabric masterEdits to Enigma's fields are saved under Enigma's names. A stock adjustment is signed metres with a reason and a description. Threshold on the inventory block is the reorder floor. One sell action per state.
Go live onlineBlocked until composition and a description are on the record; every other gate is advisory and the skips are kept. Stores are not a gate.
Swatch deskSteps in order; phase one ends at the handover to Yusuf.
Extra fabricApproval from the shelf deducts the metres from physical stock at once, alteration cloth included. Bespoke cloth, an alteration unit's ask, or a shortfall the shelf cannot cover goes to Shravani in one click from production; an alteration the shelf covers is issued and deducted on her approval; otherwise Shravani approves, the PO is generated, and it returns to production to approve when the cloth is in. Bespoke is never in inventory: nothing is booked in or deducted for it. A tool for this in Swati's hands comes in a later phase.
Washed fabricWhether a fabric is washed is set on the requirement when design raises it; its shrinkage % is entered at receiving, once a cut has been washed. Only washed fabric shrinks: stock books the usable metres after washing (received × (1 − shrinkage)); the GRN keeps the metres received.
Pre-confirmed ordersAn order not yet confirmed at the store (measurement not done, on hold, payment pending) is never planned to a factory; Allocate shows it only under the Pre-confirmed filter until someone confirms it.
Pre-productionAfter planning, every order passes CAD, cutting, fusing and compiling in that order; each step records who took it up, who finished it and when. Scan-out to the factory is refused until compiling is done.
Contrast fabricContrast fabric is inventory fabric used in small amounts on a piece. Compiling approval deducts it from physical stock; reserved stock is the main fabric held at punch, and saleable = physical − reserved − floor.
MTM productionA piece joins the queue after the freeze window. A plan over headroom is refused unless forced, and forcing is recorded with the name. Urgent sets ship-by to punch + 2 days. Scan-out deducts metres from stock and reserved. A bespoke piece is never planned before its parcel is in. A ship-by move needs a reason.
Sheet changesBefore production starts (order or PP) the store and production can correct the sheet; each correction is the next version (V2, V3). Once the piece is on a plan, measurements and styling are locked and there are no free-form change remarks; only a logistics remark can change.
StoreOnly live fabrics can be punched; a sold-out bespoke fabric cannot. Credit never above the balance; credits expire after a year. A case is raised only on a piece delivered from this store; within 30 days free alteration and credit note, after that chargeable. A full refund needs approval before finance moves it.
ReplenishmentReorder point = the larger of lead-time demand and MOQ; PO = the MOQ. Never for bespoke. Promotion to core lifts the floor to 40 m if under 20. A fabric the mill reports out goes on hold at once.
RolesA button appears only for the owning role. Store and factory roles are scoped to one store or factory. Owner sees and may do everything.
Conversation and filesOne thread per record, never deleted, readable by everyone who can open the record. A mention goes to a person or a team and stays on the bell until that person resolves it; resolving is kept on the comment. Files carry a label from the record kind's list; 25 MB per file. Nothing is emailed.
