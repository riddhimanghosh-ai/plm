2 · What the MVP builds first

The MVP is the minimum that lets each team stop its sheet. Everything marked MVP ships in the first cut; iterate comes after the pilot stores and the first RTW cohort have run on it. The order follows the build order: MTM floor, store, warehouse, requirement to PO, replenishment, RTW production, hardening.

22Writing back into Enigma where access is agreed: fabric POs, design rows, on-hold flags, GRNs, stock transfers. Phone-sized screens, bulk actions.Everyoneiterate

Rule for the cut: a feature is MVP when a team cannot stop its sheet without it. Analytics, forecasting and Enigma write-back are iterations because the teams keep working while they are built; the floor teams have no such fallback.
